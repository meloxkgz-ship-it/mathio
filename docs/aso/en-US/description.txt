WHAT MAKES MATHIO DIFFERENT

▸ PERSONAL STUDY COACH
Home recommends the best next action: clear reviews, fix missed questions, train a weak spot, finish the daily goal, continue the right lesson, or start an exam sprint.

▸ REALISTIC WEEKLY FOCUS
Mathio adapts the weekly target to the learner. A starter may begin with 3 short study days, while exam or university learners can move toward 4-5 days. Progress should feel reachable, not punishing.

▸ LONG-TERM LEARNING PATHS
Follow guided 30-, 45-, 60-, 84-, and 90-day paths for algebra, exam prep, data confidence, money math, and core mastery.

▸ STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTIONS
Wrong answer? You see the worked solution inline. No vague feedback, no guessing what went wrong.

▸ SPACED REPETITION
Overdue questions return in a Review queue using Leitner intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, then 1 month.

▸ EXAM SPRINTS
Premium learners get focused mixed practice for algebra, geometry, data, word problems, mental math, formula recall, error checking, calculator checks, and timed triage.

▸ FORMULA REFERENCE
Each lesson includes reference cards and worked examples. Bookmark formulas as you learn them.

▸ PLAIN-TEXT ANSWERS
Type answers like 6x+2, sqrt(2), or pi. No LaTeX keyboard required.

▸ PRIVACY-CONSCIOUS
Learning progress stays on your device. Mathio does not run ads or sell personal data. RevenueCat is used only for subscription entitlement validation.

▸ MULTI-LANGUAGE LEARNING
Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese, including formulas and step-by-step explanations.
